# atelier-cal.github.io

## Site content policy — 22 October 2026

- Catalogues and price PDFs are sent personally on request. Do not publish PDF catalogues or direct download links in this repository's deployed files.
- `Catalogue.html` and the four translated `catalogue.html` pages contain request forms with required email and telephone fields. Keep them `noindex, follow` and out of the sitemap.
- The Pearl 15 mm budget offer is the last machine offer on each homepage, at `#offre-petit-budget`. Legacy Pearl product pages redirect there.
- All prices are written without thousands separators (for example, `2590 € HT`).
