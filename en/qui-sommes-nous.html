Atelier CAL – Manufacturer of roller machines for professional confectionery

Experience rooted in food production and mechanical engineering

With a background in food production, I have more than 15 years of experience in the sector and a strong understanding of production, hygiene and food-processing requirements.

I trained in mechanical manufacturing and machining, then gained several years of experience in the design and development of professional machinery.

Today I design roller machines for artisan and professional confectionery production, adapted to cooked sugar and nougatine, with a direct focus on the real needs of confectioners and training centres.

Specialised in confectionery roller machines

The roller machine is at the heart of Atelier CAL. It is an essential confectionery machine used to roll, calibrate and form cooked-sugar masses consistently.

Each machine is designed to provide consistent precision, mechanical robustness and safe operation, meeting the demands of everyday workshop use.

Designed for real professional use

Materials, adjustments and components are selected to ensure reliable long-term use, straightforward maintenance and quick commissioning.

The engraved rollers, mechanical frame and overall architecture are designed to produce consistent confectionery with precise, repeatable results.

A fair-pricing approach

Atelier CAL follows a fair-pricing approach consistent with the engineering, materials and professional use of the machines, while remaining accessible to artisans and training centres.
